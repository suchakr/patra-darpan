# astro-calendar-computation-10 Notes

## Purpose

Small astronomy tranche for calendrical and computational themes: tithi,
muhūrta, Vedāṅga Jyotiṣa, yuga cycles, solar-year calculations, and planetary
positions.

## Selection

- Ten new documents, kept separate from `astro-math-indic-10`.
- All are profiled raster PDFs with Astronomy subject metadata and Indic
  category metadata.
- `Vol38_1_2_AKBag` is a related calendar paper already in the canonical
  corpus and is intentionally excluded.
