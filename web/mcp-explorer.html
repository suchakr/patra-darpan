    About MCP clients ↗

    A productive first conversation

    Ask for evidence, not just identifiers

    Tell your assistant to read the search guide and inspect coverage before searching. Ask it to cite work or paper titles with hyperlinks and verse, section or line locations.

    For metadata, use author and catalog tools first. For occurrences, use lexical search and paging. For meaning, try semantic search and verify its passages.

    Useful MCP resources: retrieval://search-guide and retrieval://ontology-context. Your assistant reads these through MCP.

    Try a conversation

    Paper metadata

    Using Sanchaya MCP, list papers by R. N. Iyengar. Use get_author_works and give readable source links. Distinguish catalog coverage from indexed content.

    Matching filenames

    Using Sanchaya MCP, search lexically for file:Jyo type:filename सूर्य. Return the filenames of matching files without content snippets.

    Ontology knowledge

    Using Sanchaya MCP, look up Dhaniṣṭhā. Show its ontology attributes and labelled relationships. Keep curated assertions separate from corpus evidence.

    Passage evidence

    Using Sanchaya MCP, find passages about Śraviṣṭhā and Dhaniṣṭhā in texts and papers. Try explicit script and spelling variants, follow continuation when available, and cite sources. Explain the limits of the search.

    Check the active release

    Ask your connected assistant to call get_corpus_info. Use its actual scope, revision and counts rather than treating this page as the deployed inventory.

    Zero does not mean absent

    Missing aliases, case endings, OCR and partial chunk coverage can yield zero entity mentions even when lexical search finds relevant text.

    Similarity is not proof

    E5’s Sanskrit quality was assessed in a small pilot. Semantic results are ranked suggestions, not a complete occurrence inventory or historical verification.

    Explore Sanskrit carefully

    Try script equivalents, plausible declensions and joined or separated sandhi forms. Treat generated forms and compound components as hypotheses; verify their sense in passages.

    Fetch only where passages are available, follow paging, and check response limits before claiming completeness. Ontology provenance is often entity-level curation, not a citation for every edge.
